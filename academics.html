STEM Programs

Northfield Institute of Technology offers career-focused programs designed to give students both theoretical knowledge and practical experience.

Computer Science

Students learn programming, software development, algorithms, databases, and computer systems.

Cybersecurity

Students study network security, system administration, digital forensics, and security principles.

Mechanical Engineering

Students study engineering design, mechanics, materials, and manufacturing while gaining hands-on experience in engineering laboratories.
