About Northfield Institute of Technology

Founded in 2008, Northfield Institute of Technology was created with a simple goal: provide students with a practical, high-quality STEM education in a small college environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to prepare students to solve complex problems through technical knowledge, creativity, and collaboration.

Our Campus

Northfield's campus includes modern classrooms, computer laboratories, engineering workshops, and science laboratories. Students have access to the tools and resources they need to put their education into practice.

Our Community

With approximately 1,500 students, Northfield provides a close-knit college environment where students can work directly with professors and build strong relationships with their classmates.

Quick Facts

  • Founded: 2008
  • Students: ~1,500
  • Student-to-faculty ratio: 12:1
  • Degrees: Bachelor's and Master's
  • Location: Northfield, Colorado
