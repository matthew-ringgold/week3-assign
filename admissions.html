Apply to Northfield

Northfield Institute of Technology welcomes students who are curious, motivated, and interested in STEM.

How to Apply

  1. Complete the online application.
  2. Submit your high school or college transcripts.
  3. Submit your application essay.
  4. Provide any required test scores.
  5. Wait for your admission decision.

What We Look For

We look for students who demonstrate academic preparation, curiosity, problem-solving skills, and a willingness to learn.

Financial Aid

Northfield offers scholarships, grants, work-study opportunities, and other forms of financial assistance to help make college more affordable.

Visit Northfield

Prospective students can schedule a campus visit to tour our facilities, meet faculty, and learn more about our programs.
